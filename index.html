Rules

The short version. The full rulebook lives in #league-rules on Discord, and staff have the final word.

Matches
  1. Matches are played on the official RSL server settings shown above.
  2. Managers schedule their match together in #manager-chat before the matchweek deadline.
  3. A draw at full time is settled by golden goal.
  4. Results are reported to staff with a screenshot of the final score.
Squads & transfers
  1. Each club can register up to 15 players.
  2. Signings and releases go through #contracts.
  3. A player can only be registered to one club at a time.
Conduct
  1. Respect opponents, staff and referees — in game and on Discord.
  2. Exploits, scripts or alt accounts lead to a ban from the league.
  3. Spam and mass mentions are removed and punished.
Managers
  1. Managers must stay active; inactive managers can be replaced.
  2. Open spots and vacant clubs are claimed through a ticket.
